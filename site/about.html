About DemoPerks

Learn how DemoPerks discovers, verifies, reviews, and presents software demo incentives.

What DemoPerks does

DemoPerks is an independent directory of publicly available business software demo incentives. It helps business buyers discover offers that may include gift cards, prepaid cards, or other rewards for completing a qualifying demonstration or sales appointment.

How offers are discovered

Listings may be found through public search results, official vendor landing pages, public advertising, community reports, and direct submissions. Social posts and advertisements may be used as discovery leads, but a listing should point to a verifiable official offer page before it is treated as active.

How offers are verified

  1. The linked page should appear to be operated by the vendor or an authorized campaign provider.
  2. The page should clearly connect a qualifying demo or appointment with a stated incentive.
  3. Past deadlines, discontinued language, contests, drawings, and unrelated educational pages should be excluded.
  4. Community feedback may trigger manual review when an offer appears broken, expired, or inaccurate.

What DemoPerks does not do

DemoPerks does not issue rewards, schedule demonstrations, determine eligibility, approve participants, or control vendor fulfillment. Each vendor sets and administers its own rules.

Sponsored and affiliate relationships

Standard listings are presented as directory information. Any sponsored placement, paid relationship, or commission-based link should be clearly labeled near the relevant listing.
