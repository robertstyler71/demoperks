About DemoPerks

An independent directory built around verification.

DemoPerks helps business software buyers find legitimate demo incentives without searching through old ads, unrelated articles, or expired promotions. Listings are gathered from public sources, checked against the vendor's landing page, and supported by community feedback.

Independent

Not the reward provider

DemoPerks does not issue gift cards, determine eligibility, schedule demonstrations, or represent the listed vendors.

Verified

Official pages first

Listings should point to an official vendor page that clearly connects a qualifying demo or appointment with a stated incentive.

Community checked

Reports trigger review

Frequently asked questions

What to know before booking a demo.

Vendor requirements can differ by role, company size, location, purchase authority, and prior customer status. Always review the official offer terms before submitting your information.

What is DemoPerks?

DemoPerks is an independent directory of publicly available business software demo incentives. It helps visitors find and compare offers but does not operate the vendor's promotion.

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. The vendor decides whether a participant qualifies and whether all attendance, identity, company, and purchase-role requirements were met. DemoPerks cannot guarantee approval, delivery, timing, or value.

Does DemoPerks issue the gift cards?

No. Rewards are administered by the listed company or its fulfillment provider. Questions about delivery should be directed to the vendor.

How are offers verified?

DemoPerks checks whether the linked page appears to be an official landing page, clearly mentions a qualifying demo or appointment, and clearly states the incentive. Automated checks can make mistakes, so listings also support visitor reports and manual review.

What does Active mean?

Active means the offer passed the most recent available verification check. It does not guarantee that the vendor has not changed, paused, restricted, or ended the promotion since that check.

Can existing customers qualify?

Often they cannot, but rules vary. Many incentives are limited to new prospects, qualified decision-makers, certain company sizes, or specific countries. Review the vendor's terms.

What happens when an offer is reported?

Issue reports are recorded and may send the listing to a manual review queue. Confirmed invalid offers may be marked expired or removed from the active directory.

Can I submit an offer?

Yes. Use the Submit an Offer form. Submissions are reviewed before listing and must follow the Submission Guidelines.

Do companies pay to be listed?

Standard listings are presented as directory information. Any future sponsored placement, paid relationship, or commission-based link should be labeled clearly near the relevant listing.

How can I request a correction or removal?

Use Report an issue on the listing. Companies and rights holders can also use the Contact information in the footer for correction, removal, privacy, or legal requests.
